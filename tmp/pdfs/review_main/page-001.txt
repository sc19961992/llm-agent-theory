宏观规律何时存在？
有限随机系统中的压缩—闭合判据
When Do Macroscopic Laws Exist? A Compression–Closure Criterion for Finite
Stochastic Systems
石琛
摘要
本文研究一个边界明确的有限状态问题：给定任务、微观观测和允许的Markov 动力学，
何时能够把大量微观状态归并为较少的宏观状态，同时保留任务并使宏观状态近似自行演
化？我们首先用稳定分区细化构造对严格冗余不变的可辨微观空间。在经典lumpability 与行
为最小化理论给出的最粗精确商基础上，本文定义允许误差时实际达到的复杂度–闭合误差
前沿。对增长系统族，我们进一步定义只依赖任务分区和纤维预测分歧的指标ΓN，并由半径–
直径夹逼证明：ΓN →0 等价于存在同一列任务保真表示，其相对状态数与最优最坏行闭合
误差同时趋零。置换对称故障–修复系统的完整前沿给出ΓN = min{1/N, (N + 1)/2N} →0；
向该核族加入一个趋于零的逐部件异质纯修复扰动，会使每个有限规模的精确任务商退化为
离散分区，却仍保持ΓN →0，从而展示近似理论相对于精确商的必要性；单点标记循环链
则满足ΓN = 1。正文还给出任务充分性与群对称两类结构证书，并明确讨论时间尺度和可
辨基线的稳健性边界；其余扩展作为条件式接口置于附录。
关键词：Markov 粗粒化；状态压缩；宏观闭合；任务保真；压缩—闭合判据；lumpability；
预测状态
English abstract. We ask when a family of finite stochastic systems admits a task-relative, low-
complexity macroscopic law. Exact observational and dynamical redundancies are first removed by
stable partition refinement. Taking the classical coarsest exact lumping as the zero-error endpoint,
we define an attained complexity–closure-error frontier and an explicit index ΓN that requires
no prespecified macroscopic transition kernel. A radius–diameter sandwich shows that ΓN →0
if and only if one sequence of task-preserving quotients has both vanishing relative state count
and vanishing optimized worst-row one-step closure defect. A permutation-invariant fault–repair
family has the exact value ΓN = min{1/N, (N + 1)/2N}. Adding a vanishing componentwise
heterogeneous pure-repair perturbation makes the exact task quotient discrete at every finite size
while preserving ΓN →0, demonstrating a genuinely approximate regime; a marked cycle instead
has ΓN = 1. We also give structural certificates based on task suﬀiciency and group symmetry
and state explicitly the time-scale and baseline-stability limits of this one-step criterion. Further
extensions are presented only as conditional interfaces.
