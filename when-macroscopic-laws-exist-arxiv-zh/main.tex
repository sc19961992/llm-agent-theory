\title{\bfseries 宏观规律何时存在？\\[0.35em]
\Large 有限随机系统中的压缩—闭合判据\\[0.8em]
\normalsize \textit{When Do Macroscopic Laws Exist? A Compression--Closure Criterion for Finite Stochastic Systems}}
\author{石琛}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
本文研究一个边界明确的有限状态问题：给定任务、微观观测和允许的 Markov 动力学，何时能够把大量微观状态归并为较少的宏观状态，同时保留任务并使宏观状态近似自行演化？我们首先构造对严格冗余不变的可辨微观空间。在该空间上，本文建立任务保真且精确闭合的唯一最粗商，以及允许误差时实际达到的复杂度--闭合误差前沿。对增长系统族，我们定义只依赖任务分区和纤维预测分歧的指标 $\Gamma_N$，并由半径--直径夹逼证明：$\Gamma_N\to0$ 等价于存在同一列任务保真表示，其相对状态数与最优最坏行闭合误差同时趋零。$\Gamma_N$ 因而给出这一存在性质的不含预设宏观转移核的等价表征。置换对称故障--修复系统提供一个非平凡正例，其完整前沿给出 $\Gamma_N=\min\{1/N,(N+1)/2^N\}\to0$；单点标记循环链满足 $\Gamma_N=1$，给出不可渐近压缩的对照族。正文进一步说明任务充分性、群对称和递推下一 token 表示如何构造候选。附录给出有限自回归模型的分布加权定理，并列出将 Transformer 应用提升为最坏情形 $\Gamma_N$ 证书所需的条件；其余附录讨论标准 Borel 空间、随机粗粒化、连续时间和范畴表述的数学接口。

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{关键词：}Markov 粗粒化；状态压缩；宏观闭合；任务保真；压缩—闭合判据；lumpability；预测状态
\end{abstract}

\begin{center}
\begin{minipage}{0.94\textwidth}
\small
\textbf{English abstract.}
We ask when a family of finite stochastic systems admits a task-relative, low-complexity macroscopic law. After quotienting exact observational and dynamical redundancies, we identify the unique coarsest task-preserving exact quotient and the attained complexity--closure-error frontier for each finite system. We then define an explicit index $\Gamma_N$, requiring no prespecified macroscopic transition kernel, and use a radius--diameter sandwich to show that $\Gamma_N\to0$ if and only if one sequence of task-preserving quotients has both vanishing relative state count and vanishing optimized worst-row one-step closure defect. A permutation-invariant fault--repair family has the exact value $\Gamma_N=\min\{1/N,(N+1)/2^N\}$, whereas a marked cycle has $\Gamma_N=1$. This characterizes asymptotically compressible, task-relative one-step closure for growing families of finite state spaces. An appendix proves a distribution-weighted result for recursive next-token representations of finite autoregressive generators and states the additional conditions required for Transformer applications; further appendices treat standard Borel factors, stochastic coarse-grainings, and continuous-time dynamics under their respective additional hypotheses.
\end{minipage}
\end{center}
