\title{\bfseries 宏观规律何时存在？\\[0.35em]
\Large 有限随机系统中的压缩—闭合判据\\[0.8em]
\normalsize \textit{When Do Macroscopic Laws Exist? A Compression--Closure Criterion for Finite Stochastic Systems}}
\author{石琛}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
本文研究一个有限状态且边界明确的问题：给定任务、微观观测和允许的 Markov 动力学，何时能够把大量微观状态归并为少量宏观状态，同时保留任务并使宏观状态近似自行演化？为避免通过复制无意义标签制造伪压缩，我们先构造对严格冗余不变的操作性微观空间。在该空间上，本文依次建立两个有限规模基准：任务保真且精确闭合的唯一最粗商，以及允许误差时实际达到的复杂度--闭合前沿。对增长系统族，我们再定义只依赖任务分区及纤维预测分歧的指标 $\Gamma_N$，并证明 $\Gamma_N\to0$ 当且仅当存在同一列任务保真表示，其相对状态数与最优最坏行闭合误差同时趋零。因而，该指标给出了本文设定下低复杂度宏观规律存在的压缩--闭合充要判据，而不是规律普遍存在的假设。可交换故障--修复系统给出非平凡正例：$2^N$ 个配置的唯一最粗精确任务商恰有 $N+1$ 个故障数状态；带单点任务的循环链则满足 $\Gamma_N=1$，说明有限性本身不保证宏观简化。正文最后说明残余对称和 Transformer 预测状态如何提供候选表示；Transformer 的完整分布加权构造保留在附录，并明确列出其成为最坏情形 $\Gamma_N$ 证书所需的额外对齐条件。标准 Borel 空间、随机粗粒化、连续时间、相结构和范畴表述也完整保留在附录，但不被写成有限主定理已经证明的推广。

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{关键词：}Markov 粗粒化；状态压缩；宏观闭合；任务保真；压缩—闭合判据；lumpability；预测状态
\end{abstract}

\begin{center}
\begin{minipage}{0.94\textwidth}
\small
\textbf{English abstract.}
We ask when a family of finite stochastic systems admits a task-relative, low-complexity macroscopic law. After quotienting exact operational redundancies, we identify the unique coarsest task-preserving exact quotient and the attained complexity--closure frontier for each finite system. We then define an explicit index $\Gamma_N$, requiring no prespecified macroscopic transition kernel, and prove that $\Gamma_N\to0$ if and only if one sequence of task-preserving quotients has both vanishing relative state count and vanishing optimized worst-row one-step closure defect. A permutation-invariant fault--repair family gives a nontrivial exact example with $2^N$ microscopic configurations and a minimal $(N+1)$-state quotient, whereas a marked cycle has $\Gamma_N=1$. Transformer predictive states and several mathematical extensions are retained as scoped appendices. The criterion is task-relative and one-step; it does not assert universal simplicity, efficient discovery, causal autonomy, or long-horizon accuracy.
\end{minipage}
\end{center}
